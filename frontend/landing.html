The problem

Promises go out. Money never follows.

Phantom refunds

The agent says “refund processed.” The ledger says $0.00. The customer waits ten days, then chargebacks.

Policy breaches

Full refunds on final-sale items, over-limit amounts, duplicate credits. Found at month-end close, if ever.

Silent cancellations

“Cancelled” in chat, still billing in Stripe. Next cycle triggers the dispute.

How it works

Pull each promised dollar, then show the Stripe record or its absence.

    Connect with test-mode keys

    Bring a transcript export and test-mode Stripe and Shopify keys. Nothing writes until you approve a named order.

  2. 02

    Reconcile up to 90 days

    Every AI-resolved ticket you provide is reconciled into promised dollars against moved dollars. That is the leakage ledger.

  3. 03

    Refund the exact shortfall

    One click retries the shortfall with an idempotent key, so a double click cannot double refund. Each drift carries its transcript quote and the ledger record checked.

Objections

Objections, answered

Can't a frontier model just do this?

We tested it. Same 12 cases, same prompt: an open-weight model and Gemini 4 Argon both matched 11 of 12 entities. The difference was how they failed. Argon invented an order number that appears nowhere in the transcript; ours returned nothing. A fabricated id can reconcile against the wrong order and report a confident answer about someone else's money. Model cost is about $0.0096 per audited transcript, roughly a third of the Opus tier for the same input shape. Cheaper models exist. They drop fields. Read the comparison

Why not just switch to a better agent?

Every agent drifts. Air Canada and Priceline proved the premise. Switching agents changes which agent you verify, never whether verification is needed.

Why not vendor guardrails or audit trails?

Guardrails prevent before send for one vendor. Trails log tool calls without checking ledger truth. Settleye verifies after execution, across vendors.

What stops Shopify or Stripe from building it?

They verify their own rails and never read transcripts. Neutrality is the product.

Is the model decorative?

No. The demo shows raw transcript to raw model JSON to diff with no cuts. A wire-level test pins the model ID on every request.
