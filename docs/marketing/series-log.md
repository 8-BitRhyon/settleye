# Series Log: the Thinking Behind Settleye

Prompt-by-prompt record of the human decisions in this build. Each entry is one post seed. Written for builders, in the owner voice.

## 1. Start with gaps, not features

First instruction: build fast, surface gaps after every build, flag anything only I can do personally. The agent owns code, research, verification, and repo hygiene. I own credentials, test orders, video, and submission. That split never changed.

## 2. Rename on day one or pay forever

Spotted a same-name app in our exact marketplace. Verdict: rename now while it costs one commit. Lesson: distribution channels decide names, not taste.

## 3. Test orders are real work

Bogus gateways, draft checkouts, manual marks. Unsexy clicks that unblock every live proof after them. Skipping them is how demos stay mocked.

## 4. Ask whether it is enough, out loud

"Is this enough to push the frontier?" The honest answer was no: pipes without packaging do not win. That question created the eval set, the numbers, and everything after.

## 5. Steal workflows across harnesses

Antigravity has /goal. OpenCode does not. So we emulated it: no premature stops, evidence-mapped completion, run until done. Plus /no-mistakes as the gate. Good workflows are portable. Loyal to outcomes, not to tools.

## 6. Audit your own skill shelf

Checked the monozen-skills folder for hackathon-relevant skills instead of assuming. Found the verifier, the loop, the audit. The shelf you already own beats the tool you have not installed.

## 7. Score yourself against the rubric early

Looked up the actual weights (Impact 30, Tooling 25, Demo 25, Depth 20) and scored the project honestly per line. Demo sat at zero. That decided the next two weeks.

## 8. Style is a linter, not a vibe

"No M dashes" became an enforced skill plus a lint script over every living doc. Taste scales when it compiles.

## 9. Let Gemini do what it is good at

Two deep-research runs: strategy, then impact numbers. My job was writing tight prompts and integrating answers with qualifiers attached. Research is outsourcing curiosity with a return address.

## 10. Freeze features like a judge

The Rashad playbook says most points come from the pitch. Adopted it as a skill, ran the audit, froze features. Three heroes, everything else is packaging or pitch.

## 11. Scope before pixels

Landing page started as a design doc from three parallel research tracks, not a mockup. Type, color, and structure each got a verdict before any code. Argued dark versus white for a full round. Lost 3-0 to the references. Updated the doc instead of my ego.

## 12. Study what you love, steal what fits

memoe, Collar, Global Citizens, Jimo, Bond. Each got a screenshot and a steal-list with explicit rejections. Taste without a filter is moodboarding. The filter was: CSS-only, token-compatible, content-honest, judge-legible.

## 13. Inter is the template

Called out the most-used font in tech as a liability. Studied my own repo to remember my actual dialect. Landed on machine grotesk plus system plus mono. Distinctive within a perf budget.

## 14. Cost discipline is a feature

Wonder for taste, Contributor at 40x cheaper for mechanical swaps. Models are line items. Spend taste where judges look.

## 15. The brand sat in the name

Settleye always contained the third eye. The pin image gave it a face: mint core, star glint, marbled iris. Presentation won over the perf ceiling, formally: floor 85, accessibility 100.

## 16. Fix your mistakes in public (then scrub them)

The build log claimed day two and three for work done in one day. Fixed it, then scrubbed the mistake from history. Ship the correction, keep the humor.

## 17. Ask if you are normal, accept the answer

Abnormal execution, normal day-one position. Volume is not differentiation. Discipline is: test-first, live-verified, insultingly honest docs.

## 18. Replicate soloists, not teams

Levels for distribution-as-building, solo hackathon winners for scope discipline. One audience, every launch. Build first, then narrate. The series is the audience asset.

## 19. Customers are not required, conversations are

No traction rule exists in the criteria. One quotable merchant conversation beats ten cold features. Five DMs, one reply, back to the video.

## 20. Credits are supply lines, not strategy

$150 in Meta credits cannot run our extraction model (not API-served) and Wonder costs 10x for a solved job. Product stays. Credits fund experiments. Novelty never overrules numbers.

## 21. File research, freeze building

Gemini returned the Jev plus agentic-commerce deep track. Filed it as `16`, recorded the verdict in design.md: coherent, premature, no key, no metered trial, freeze holds. Research compounds. Code waits.
