# Extensible Marketing Plan

Designed to be copied per launch, per channel, per merchant segment. Cadence survives on 30 minutes a day.

## Channels in priority order

1. X (Most Viral $20k + community vote halo). Daily during judging week, 3x weekly after.
2. Hackathon gallery plus Discord (votes, feedback, design partners).
3. Shopify/e-commerce communities (merchant pain threads; never spam, answer refund questions with the phantom pattern and link the eval).
4. Email outreach to mid-market ops leads (design partners, not blasts; 5 per week).

## Content pillars (rotate, never repeat twice in a row)

1. Drift of the week: one anonymized phantom pattern with dollars (e.g. "Agent promised $45, ledger $0, chargeback day 11").
2. Receipts: eval numbers, false-all-clear rate, inbox precision, benchmark drops.
3. Build log: what shipped, with the commit linked. Judges and devs both read these.
4. Regulation watch: one-line tailwind (EU fines, VAMP clock, 13.6 grace) plus what it means for merchants.
5. Merchant math: tier ROI worked examples from `07-tangible-value.md`.

## Copyable weekly cadence

- Monday: drift of the week (pillar 1).
- Wednesday: build log or receipts (pillars 3/2).
- Friday: merchant math or regulation watch (pillars 5/4).
- Daily during Nov 5-8: one demo clip (under 30 seconds) plus the gallery link.

## Templates

- Launch post, follow-up stat reply, video description, submission blurb, partner DM, bump email: see `launch-kit.md`. Copy verbatim first, customize second.
- Landing page: see `landing-copy.md`. Every claim on it must already be true in the repo.

## Rules

- Every public number must exist in `docs/research/` with a source URL first.
- Never publish a merchant name without written permission. Anonymize to segment plus GMV band.
- Never claim a model capability we have not demonstrated on camera.
- Engagement is a lagging indicator of useful artifacts: eval drops, fixture packs, and teardown posts outperform announcements 10 to 1. Ship the artifact, then announce it.

## Metrics (review Fridays)

- X: profile visits, demo video starts, repo stars, gallery votes.
- Pipeline: shadow audits requested, leakage ledgers delivered, design-partner calls.
- Product: false-all-clear rate, inbox precision, weekly drift dollars found across pilots.
