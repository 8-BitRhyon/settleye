# Gemini Deep Research Prompt: Impact Quantification for Settleye

Paste everything below the line into Gemini Deep Research. Goal is advertising and distribution ammo: sourced numbers that make the impact case for an execution auditor. Do not re-research product strategy (covered in `09-gemini-deep-research-prompt.md`).

---

Research impact numbers for Settleye: an execution auditor for AI customer-support agents. It extracts binding money promises (refunds) from support transcripts and reconciles each against Stripe and Shopify ledger state. Buyers are e-commerce merchants and support ops leads running AI agents. Price anchor is $99/mo against about $494/mo in measured value for mid-market.

## What we already know (do not re-research)

- FTC Sentinel: $12.5B fraud losses 2024 (up 25%), 38% of reporters lost money.
- All-in dispute cost $128 (Mastercard 2025, before goods). Merchants pay $15 to $40 to kill one dispute (Ethoca and RDR).
- Precedents: Air Canada chatbot tribunal (carrier honored invented policy), Priceline bot refunds on non-refundable bookings.
- EU AI Act Art 50 transparency duties live Aug 2026. CA AB 2863 live.
- Our open eval: 12 of 12 verdicts correct. Zero non-binding drifts in four chances.
- No published promised-but-unissued refund rate exists (confirmed gap).

## Questions (priority order)

1. Refund leakage: find any measured rate of refunds promised but never issued, refund-related chargeback share, or first-contact-refund failure rates. Support ops benchmarks (SQM, ICMI, Zendesk, Forrester) count. Mark gaps as UNVERIFIED, never fill them.
2. AI agent adoption: how many e-commerce merchants run AI support agents in 2026? Which helpdesks (Gorgias, Kustomer, Decagon, Sierra, Forethought) dominate that segment? Size the reachable buyer pool.
3. Chargeback math: current average chargeback cost per incident (fee plus goods plus labor). Compare representment win rates with vs without transcript evidence. State Visa CE3.0 field requirements in one paragraph.
4. Buyer proof: what evidence do support ops buyers require before paying for audit tooling (pilot shape, ROI format, security review)? Cite adjacent vendor case studies with numbers.
5. Distribution: which channels actually reach e-commerce ops buyers (communities, newsletters, podcasts, app stores, agency partners)? Name names with audience sizes where public.
6. Ad angles: five headline-ready claims a founder can make on camera, each backed by one sourced number from above. Flag any claim that needs a qualifier.

## Deliverable format

Return three parts. First, a ranked list of the 10 strongest sourced facts, each with a source URL and date. Second, the three ad angles with the hardest numbers behind them. Third, the single biggest UNVERIFIED gap and the cheapest way to measure it (survey, pilot clause, public dataset). Prefer primary docs, filings, and measured benchmarks. Reject vendor marketing without independent backing.
